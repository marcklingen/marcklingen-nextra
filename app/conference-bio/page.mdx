Marc Klingen co-founded [Langfuse](https://langfuse.com) (YC W23), the open-source platform for tracing, evaluating, and improving LLM applications and AI agents. Today, Langfuse is [used by more than 50,000 companies](https://langfuse.com/customers), including 21 of the Fortune 50, and its SDKs are installed over 50 million times each month. Following Langfuse’s [acquisition by ClickHouse](https://langfuse.com/blog/joining-clickhouse) in 2026, Marc continues to lead the Langfuse team and product. He works closely with developers, open-source contributors, and customers to build better tools for reliable, production-grade AI systems.
